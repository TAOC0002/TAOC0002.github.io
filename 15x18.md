# 15-418/618 Final Project -- Simulating Stellar Cluster Evolution in Parallel

## URL

[https://taoc0002.github.io/15x18/](https://taoc0002.github.io/15x18/)

## Author

Chen Tao (chentao) and Hasita Kanamarlapudi (hkanamar)

## Summary

We plan to accelerate star cluster evolution simulation through parallelism on CPUs (OpenMP) and GPUs (CUDA), and evaluate their performances compared to a single-thread baseline. We target a star cluster of 100k to 1M stars where gravity moves the stars and each star
ages along its own life track.
